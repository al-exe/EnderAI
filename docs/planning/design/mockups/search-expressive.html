how do we handle stripe tax for new subscribers?
Read 1 document, 3 anchors · Stripe checkout wiring §1 §3 §5
TF

On signup we attach automatic_tax: { enabled: true } to every subscription. For business accounts we additionally pass tax_id_collection so customers can input a VAT ID at checkout§1.

Edge case: EU customers with no VAT are blocked at confirm — our fallback switches them to the B2C plan§3. The full rationale (and why we didn't build a custom tax engine) is in the tax-engine decision§5.

EU customer, no VAT
When a customer in an EU country selects the business plan without a valid VAT ID, Stripe blocks the confirm with tax_id_invalid. Our fallback switches the customer to the B2C plan with gross-of-tax pricing and surfaces a non-blocking notice.
